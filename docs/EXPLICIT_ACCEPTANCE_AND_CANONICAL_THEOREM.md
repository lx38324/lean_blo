# Explicit proposal acceptance and canonical end-to-end theorem

This note records the final certificate-facing closure used by the highest-level
OUSVR-BLO theorem.  The purpose is to remove three remaining abstractions from
the public statement:

1. the accept/fallback choice is generated by explicit certificate tests;
2. the baseline error premise uses the actual base residual, not the enlarged
   envelope;
3. the Young-balance scale and step norm are definitions, not independent
   assumptions.

The result remains a restricted/local fixed-penalty value-surrogate theorem.

## 1. Certificate-generated accept/fallback rule

At iteration `t`, let

- `Rbase_t` be the residual of the safe base response;
- `Rprop_t` be the residual of the learned proposal;
- `eB_t` and `eProp_t` be their true value-gradient errors;
- `ehatB_t` and `ehatProp_t` be computable proxy errors.

Define the uncertainty-adjusted proposal margin

$$
M_t
=
\widehat\Delta_t-\tau_t^e-\rho_t^{\rm prop}-\rho_t^B.
$$

The proposal is accepted exactly when all three tests hold:

$$
\mathcal A_t
:\Longleftrightarrow
\begin{cases}
R_t^{\rm prop}\le R_t^B+\tau_t^R,\\[1mm]
\widehat e_t^{\rm prop}
\le
\widehat e_t^B-\widehat\Delta_t+\tau_t^e,\\[1mm]
M_t\ge0.
\end{cases}
$$

The selected residual, true error, and certified gain are

$$
R_t^O
=
\begin{cases}
R_t^{\rm prop},&\mathcal A_t,\\
R_t^B,&\neg\mathcal A_t,
\end{cases}
$$

$$
e_t^O
=
\begin{cases}
e_t^{\rm prop},&\mathcal A_t,\\
e_t^B,&\neg\mathcal A_t,
\end{cases}
$$

and

$$
\Gamma_t
=
\begin{cases}
M_t,&\mathcal A_t,\\
0,&\neg\mathcal A_t.
\end{cases}
$$

## 2. Fallback-safe residual envelope

Assume the safe base response satisfies

$$
R_t^B
\le
(1-\theta)R_t+\varepsilon_t^B,
$$

and define

$$
Q_t:=R_t^B+\tau_t^R,
\qquad
\varepsilon_t:=\varepsilon_t^B+\tau_t^R.
$$

If the proposal is accepted, the first acceptance test gives

$$
R_t^O=R_t^{\rm prop}\le Q_t.
$$

If it is rejected, then

$$
R_t^O=R_t^B\le Q_t
$$

because `tauR_t >= 0`.  Therefore in both cases

$$
\boxed{R_t^O\le Q_t.}
$$

Moreover,

$$
Q_t
=R_t^B+\tau_t^R
\le
(1-\theta)R_t+\varepsilon_t^B+\tau_t^R
=(1-\theta)R_t+\varepsilon_t.
$$

Thus the abstract contraction interface is generated by the actual selection
rule.

## 3. True-error improvement in both branches

Assume proxy calibration

$$
|\widehat e_t^{\rm prop}-e_t^{\rm prop}|
\le\rho_t^{\rm prop},
\qquad
|\widehat e_t^B-e_t^B|
\le\rho_t^B.
$$

On an accepted round,

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_t^O
&=e_t^{\rm prop}
\le \widehat e_t^{\rm prop}+\rho_t^{\rm prop}\\
&\le \widehat e_t^B-\widehat\Delta_t+\tau_t^e
  +\rho_t^{\rm prop}\\
&\le e_t^B+\rho_t^B-\widehat\Delta_t+\tau_t^e
  +\rho_t^{\rm prop}\\
&=e_t^B-\Gamma_t.
\end{aligned}
$$

On a rejected round,

$$
e_t^O=e_t^B,
\qquad
\Gamma_t=0,
$$

so the same inequality holds exactly.  Hence

$$
\boxed{e_t^O\le e_t^B-\Gamma_t,\qquad \Gamma_t\ge0.}
$$

## 4. Baseline residual bound in its natural form

The top-level theorem now permits the natural base-response premise

$$
e_t^B\le C_RR_t^B+b_t.
$$

Since

$$
R_t^B\le Q_t
$$

and `C_R > 0`, monotonicity gives

$$
e_t^B
\le C_RR_t^B+b_t
\le C_RQ_t+b_t.
$$

Combining with the selected true-error comparison yields

$$
\boxed{
e_t^O
\le
C_RQ_t+b_t-\Gamma_t.
}
$$

If the inexact-gradient vector satisfies

$$
\|E_t\|^2\le\lambda^2e_t^O,
$$

then

$$
\boxed{
\|E_t\|^2
\le
\lambda^2(C_RQ_t+b_t-\Gamma_t).
}
$$

## 5. Residual drift starts from the selected response

A more direct local residual-smoothness premise is

$$
R_{t+1}
\le
R_t^O
+\langle \nabla R_t,\Delta x_t\rangle
+\frac{L_R}{2}\|\Delta x_t\|^2+d_t.
$$

Because the selector proves `Ronline_t <= Q_t`, this implies

$$
R_{t+1}
\le
Q_t
+\langle \nabla R_t,\Delta x_t\rangle
+\frac{L_R}{2}\|\Delta x_t\|^2+d_t.
$$

Thus the theorem no longer asks the user to replace the accepted residual by
`Q_t` manually.

## 6. Canonical Young scale and step norm

Define

$$
H_t:=\sqrt{C_RQ_t+b_t}.
$$

The residual envelope and bias are nonnegative, so

$$
C_RQ_t+b_t\ge0,
\qquad
H_t\ge0,
\qquad
H_t^2=C_RQ_t+b_t.
$$

Define the actual inexact step norm

$$
s_t:=\|G_t+E_t\|.
$$

The standard inequality

$$
\|G_t+E_t\|^2
\le
2\|G_t\|^2+2\|E_t\|^2
$$

is proved in Lean from the triangle inequality and

$$
(\|G_t\|-\|E_t\|)^2\ge0.
$$

Consequently `H_t`, `s_t`, their nonnegativity, the identity for `H_t^2`, and
the squared-step estimate are no longer independent top-level fields.

## 7. Canonical finite-horizon theorem

Let the exact manuscript drift parameters be

$$
\mu=\frac1{\sqrt2\lambda},
$$

$$
A_\eta
=
\frac{\eta}{2\sqrt2\lambda}
+
\frac{L_R\eta^2}{2},
$$

$$
\beta_\eta
=
\sqrt2\lambda\eta
+
\lambda^2L_R\eta^2.
$$

Assume

$$
C_R\beta_\eta\le\frac\theta4,
\qquad
0<\theta\le1.
$$

The complete selector-facing chain yields

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\frac\eta4\sum_{t<T}\|G_t\|^2
+C_\Gamma\sum_{t<T}\Gamma_t
+\frac{\eta\lambda^2C_R}{4}\sum_{t<T}R_t\\
&\le
\Psi_0-P_\star
+C_\varepsilon\sum_{t<T}\varepsilon_t
+C_b\sum_{t<T}b_t
+C_d\sum_{t<T}d_t,
\end{aligned}
}
$$

where

$$
C_\Gamma
=
\frac{\eta\lambda^2}{2}
+2\alpha A_\eta\lambda^2,
$$

and Lean proves

$$
\frac{\eta\lambda^2}{2}
\le C_\Gamma
\le\frac34\eta\lambda^2.
$$
